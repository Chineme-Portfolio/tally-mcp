Consequence of the production guard: the production rollback is **reverting the code**, not disabling auth. Follow ups queued: `foundation.md` v7 (§7 #6 superseded), revisit §7 #14 / `code-standards.md` §9 now that Tally holds PII and tokens, **update `code-standards.md` §5** (it currently mandates the `resolveUserId()` rule this spec reverses), self serve deletion, and refresh the stale `build-graph.md`.
